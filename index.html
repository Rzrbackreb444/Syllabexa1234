Syllabexa Enterprise AI Publishing & Ghostwriting Suite

What is Syllabexa?

Syllabexa is an elite AI-powered publishing engine and ghostwriting suite that transforms raw writing samples, transcripts, or notes into fully structured, voice-consistent books. Designed for professional authors, publishers, and B2B ghostwriters.

Core Capabilities

  • Voice Training & Cloning: Extract author stylistic fingerprints and clone custom narrator voices using ElevenLabs integration.
  • Multi-Model AI Pipeline: Orchestrate chapter generation, beat sheets, and automated editorial reviews with zero context loss.
  • Atticus & Vellum Typesetting Studio: Live preview PDF and EPUB layouts with classic, academic, luxury, and blueprint typography themes, drop caps, and custom CSS overrides.
  • Audiobook Production Studio: Batch-apply ID3 metadata tags, series titles, book numbers, and generate synchronized audiobook chapter files.
  • Distribution & Publishing Funnel: KDP, IngramSpark, and Apple Books export planning with automated compliance checks.
